# Rule 4 - Outreach compliance

LeadShoot finds and ranks; it does not send. When the user moves to outreach:

- **CAN-SPAM applies to all commercial email - there is no B2B exemption.**
  Any email help must include: truthful from/subject, the sender's physical
  postal address, and a working opt-out that is honored promptly.
- Prefer phone-first outreach for local businesses (it converts better and
  the numbers are owner-published). The pitch must reference the *verified*
  gap - honest specificity is the whole advantage.
- Never fabricate urgency or problems: if the gap is unverified, the pitch
  says "I couldn't find your website" (invitation to correct), not "your
  website is broken" (claim).
- One pitch per business per campaign; a lead marked `lost` or `hidden` is
  closed - do not suggest re-contacting it.
- No review-gating, fake reviews, or "we'll remove bad reviews" pitches when
  using review signals - reputation help means earning reviews, responding
  professionally, and fixing service issues.
